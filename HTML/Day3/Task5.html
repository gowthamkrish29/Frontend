The Purpose of Structural Architecture

Semantic layouts swap generic dividers for descriptive elements. This ensures search engines and machines can parse your core text immediately without running into presentation guesswork.

By defining exactly where content begins and ends, your page remains stable, easy to maintain, and backward-compatible across older browsers.
