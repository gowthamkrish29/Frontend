Education in Madhavaram

Education plays an important role in the development of Madhavaram Assembly Constituency. The constituency has several Government Schools, Private Schools, Colleges and Skill Development Centres that provide quality education to thousands of students every year.


Government Schools

Government schools provide affordable and quality education to students from different backgrounds. They also offer free textbooks, uniforms, mid-day meals and scholarship schemes.
