Government Offices in Madhavaram

Madhavaram Assembly Constituency has several government offices that provide essential public services to residents. These offices help citizens obtain certificates, utility services, law enforcement, civic administration and other government-related assistance.


Revenue & Taluk Office

The Revenue and Taluk Office is responsible for land administration and issuing various government certificates to citizens.

Services Provided

  • Community Certificate
  • Income Certificate
  • Nativity Certificate
  • Legal Heir Certificate
  • Patta and Chitta Services
  • Land Records

Greater Chennai Corporation Office

The Greater Chennai Corporation is responsible for maintaining civic infrastructure and delivering municipal services within the constituency.

Corporation Services

  1. Birth Certificate
  2. Death Certificate
  3. Property Tax Collection
  4. Solid Waste Management
  5. Street Light Maintenance
  6. Road Maintenance
  7. Drainage Services

Police Department

Police stations maintain law and order, ensure public safety and provide emergency assistance to citizens.

Police Services

  • Complaint Registration
  • Traffic Management
  • Crime Prevention
  • Women's Safety
  • Emergency Response

Fire and Rescue Services

Fire and Rescue Services provide emergency response during fire accidents, rescue operations and disaster management.

  • Fire Emergency Response
  • Rescue Operations
  • Disaster Management
  • Public Safety Awareness

India Post Office

The Post Office offers postal, banking and financial services to the public.

  • Speed Post
  • Registered Post
  • Savings Account
  • Money Transfer
  • Parcel Services
