Welcome to Madhavaram Assembly Constituency

Introduction

Madhavaram Assembly Constituency is one of the important Assembly constituencies in Chennai district, Tamil Nadu. It has experienced rapid residential, commercial, and industrial growth over the past decade. The constituency is known for excellent road connectivity, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and public services. It includes several developing residential neighborhoods and plays a significant role in the development of North Chennai.

The constituency is represented by an elected Member of the Legislative Assembly (MLA), who works with various government departments to improve infrastructure, education, healthcare, transportation, and public welfare.

Madhavaram serves as a strategic northern gateway to Chennai city, offering seamless connectivity via the Grand Northern Trunk (GNT) Road (NH 16) and the massive Madhavaram Moffussil Bus Terminus (MMBT). The constituency is currently undergoing an unprecedented urban transformation with the rapid expansion of the Chennai Metro Rail (CMRL Phase 2), positioning it as a vital multi-modal transport and commercial hub for the entire state.
