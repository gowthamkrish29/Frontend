My Personal Favorites



Favorite Food: Schezwan Fried Rice

A spicy, vibrant, and incredibly flavorful Indo-Chinese dish made by tossing cooked rice with fiery Schezwan sauce, crisp vegetables, and aromatic spices.

  • Key Ingredients: Basmati rice, garlic, ginger, celery, soy sauce, and signature fiery red Schezwan chili paste.
  • Taste Profile: Intensely spicy, pungent, tangy, and loaded with savory umami flavors.
  • Pairing: Tastes absolutely best when served piping hot alongside Veg Manchurian or Chilli Chicken.


Favorite Movie: Shutter Island

A brilliant psychological thriller directed by Martin Scorsese, following US Marshal Teddy Daniels as he investigates the disappearance of a patient from a hospital for the criminally insane.

  • Genre: Psychological thriller, neo-noir, mystery, and psychological horror.
  • Release Year: Premiered globally in February 2010 to massive critical acclaim.
  • Why it's great: Features a legendary plot twist, dark atmospheric tension, and deeply haunting musical scores.


Favorite Place: Manali

A breathtaking, high-altitude Himalayan resort town nestled in India’s northern Himachal Pradesh state, renowned for its snow-capped peaks and vibrant culture.

  • Attractions: Famous for Solang Valley adventures, Rohtang Pass snow views, and peaceful cedar forest walks.
  • Activities: Perfect destination for paragliding, river rafting, mountain trekking, and relaxing in hot springs.
  • Vibe: A scenic mix of serene mountain landscapes, cozy wooden cabins, and bustling local markets.


Favorite Actor: Leonardo DiCaprio

One of the finest global actors of his generation, widely recognized for his intense, immersive performances and dedication to complex, dramatic roles.

  • Iconic Movies: Starred in masterpieces like Shutter Island, Inception, Titanic, and The Wolf of Wall Street.
  • Academy Awards: Won the prestigious Best Actor Oscar in 2016 for his survival role in The Revenant.
  • Acting Style: Famous for emotional intensity, deep psychological character transformations, and collaborating with top directors.


Favorite Sport: Cricket

A globally celebrated bat-and-ball team sport that demands strategy, physical athleticism, immense focus, and precise team synchronization.

  • Formats: Played across three main variations including Test Matches, One Day Internationals (ODIs), and fast T20s.
  • Core Skills: Requires masterclass batting precision, tactical bowling variations, and lightning-fast fielding reflexes.
  • Global Appeal: Boasts a massive fan following with historic tournaments like the ICC World Cup and local leagues.
