Accenture plc is one of the world’s largest global professional services and management consulting firms, providing data, technology, and operational overhauls to organizations worldwide.

The company originated as the consulting arm of the accounting firm Arthur Andersen in the 1950s. It formally separated, rebranded as Accenture in 2001, and is currently headquartered in Dublin, Ireland. Globally led by Chair and CEO Julie Sweet, Accenture operates in over 120 countries with a workforce of more than 774,000 employees.

Accenture consolidates its main capabilities into streamlined, AI-driven umbrellas:Strateg

  • Strategy & Consulting — Designing corporate transformations, supply chain overhauls, and industry-specific scaling models.
  • Technology Services — Cloud migration, systems integration, application management, and large-scale cybersecurity implementations.
  • Operations — Managing client business processes, data centers, payroll networks, and customer support infrastructure.
  • Song & Industry X — Delivering digital marketing, brand experiences, product design, and advanced industrial engineering systems.
  • I & Data Studio Deployments — Driving rapid enterprise changes through global Generative AI studios and agentic AI models.
