Accenture plc is a leading global professional services company. Its localized corporate entity in India operates under the name Accenture Solutions Private Limited



About Company

History: Founded in 1989 as Andersen Consulting, it rebranded to Accenture in 2001. It is legally domiciled in Dublin, Ireland.

Scale: The company employs hundreds of thousands of professionals serving clients across more than 120 countries.

Leadership: The organization is led globally by Chair and CEO Julie Sweet.

India Presence: Accenture in India serves as a core hub for the firm's global operations, maintaining a massive workforce presence across major tech hubs like Bengaluru, Chennai, Pune, Gurugram, Mumbai, and Hyderabad.



Services

Strategy & Consulting: Business strategy, technology strategy, operations strategy, and comprehensive management consulting.

Technology: Systems integration, cloud architecture, application transformation, data analytics, cyber security, and large-scale Generative AI implementation.

Operations: Business Process Outsourcing (BPO), infrastructure engineering, cloud management, and intelligent operational services.

Accenture Song: Specialized digital experience, creative strategy, customer analytics, and commerce solutions.
