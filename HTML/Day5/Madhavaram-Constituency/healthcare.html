Healthcare Services in Madhavaram

Healthcare is one of the essential public services available in Madhavaram Assembly Constituency. The constituency has several Government Hospitals, Primary Health Centres, Private Hospitals, Clinics, Diagnostic Centres and Medical Shops that provide quality healthcare services to residents.


Government Healthcare Facilities

Government healthcare institutions provide affordable medical treatment, emergency care, maternity services, vaccination programmes and public health initiatives for citizens.

Available Government Facilities

  • Government Hospital, Madhavaram
  • Urban Primary Health Centre
  • Government Health Sub Centres
  • Imunization Centres
  • Mother and Child Care Services

Private Hospitals and Clinics

Private hospitals provide advanced medical facilities, specialist consultations, emergency care, diagnostic services and surgical treatments.

Major Private Hospitals (Nearby)

  1. Apollo Clinic
  2. Be Well Hospital
  3. Frontier Lifeline Hospital
  4. KVT Health Care
  5. Multispeciality Hospitals in North Chennai

Healthcare Services Available

Hospitals and health centres provide treatment for patients of all age groups with modern medical equipment and qualified healthcare professionals.

  • General Medicine
  • Emergency Care
  • Cardiology
  • Orthopaedics
  • Pediatrics
  • Gynecology
  • Dental Care
  • Diagnostic Laboratory

Emergency Healthcare Services

Emergency healthcare facilities are available throughout the constituency to provide immediate medical assistance.

  1. 108 Ambulance Service
  2. 24×7 Emergency Hospitals
  3. Blood Banks
  4. Diagnostic Laboratories
  5. Medical Shops
  6. Intensive Care Units (ICU)

Public Health Initiatives

The Tamil Nadu Government conducts several public health programmes to improve the overall well-being of citizens.

  • Free Medical Camps
  • Vaccination Drives
  • Blood Donation Camps
  • Maternal Healthcare
  • Child Nutrition Programmes
  • Health Awareness Campaigns

Good healthcare services improve the quality of life and ensure a healthier future for every citizen.
