Who Can Vote?

Every Indian citizen who satisfies the election eligibility criteria can participate in the democratic process by voting.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be an Indian Citizen.
  • Must be 18 years of age or above.
  • Name should be included in the Electoral Roll.
  • Carry a valid Voter ID or approved identity proof.

Importance of Voting

Voting is one of the most important rights of every citizen. It allows people to choose responsible leaders who can work towards the development of society and improve public welfare.

  • Strengthens Democracy
  • Promotes Good Governance
  • Encourages Citizen Participation
  • Supports Transparent Administration
  • Ensures Equal Representation
