1. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Focuses on programming, software development, and computer applications. Students learn languages like C, Java, Python, and web technologies. Career opportunities include Software Developer, Full Stack Developer, Data Analyst, and AI Engineer.



2. Information Technology (IT)

Students learn cybersecurity, cloud computing, and web technologies. Focuses on managing computer systems, networks, and databases. Career opportunities include System Administrator, Network Engineer, IT Support, and Cloud Engineer.



3. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)

Focuses on electronic circuits, communication systems, and embedded systems. Students learn digital electronics, microprocessors, and wireless communication. Career opportunities include Electronics Engineer, Embedded Systems Engineer, and Telecom Engineer.



4. Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)

Focuses on electrical power generation, transmission, and distribution. Students learn electrical machines, power systems, and control systems. Career opportunities include Electrical Engineer, Power Plant Engineer, and Maintenance Engineer.



5. Mechanical Engineering

Focuses on the design, manufacturing, and maintenance of machines. Students learn thermodynamics, manufacturing processes, and CAD software. Career opportunities include Design Engineer, Production Engineer, and Automotive Engineer.
